# Content Strategy & Publishing Cadence

## Publishing Objectives
1. Provide daily high-signal engineering and AI architecture breakdowns.
2. Build permanent authority in autonomous systems, AI workflows, and software reliability.
3. Repurpose core technical insights into platform-native formats without copy-pasting.

## Publishing Schedule & Cadence
- **Frequency:** 1 to 2 batches per day.
  - Batch 1 (Morning): 10:00 Europe/Bucharest (Educational or Industry News Breakdown)
  - Batch 2 (Late Afternoon): 17:00 Europe/Bucharest (Practical Tutorial, Thought Leadership, or Case Study)
- **Time Randomization:** Apply a +/- 15 to 30 minute jitter window to simulate natural posting patterns.
- **Deduplication Window:** 30 days lookback to prevent repeating topics, angles, or opening hooks.

## Platform Mix
- **X (Twitter):** Single clear insight, concise hook, no fluff, actionable takeaway, max 2-3 hashtags.
- **LinkedIn:** Professional perspective, technical trade-offs, architecture design, conversational discussion question.
- **Instagram:** Visually compelling caption, short paragraphs, line breaks, technical key points, 5-8 relevant tags.
- **Facebook:** Accessible tech breakdown, community discussion prompt, natural CTA.
- **YouTube:** Full video script, SEO title, comprehensive description with timestamps/chapters, tags, thumbnail concept.
