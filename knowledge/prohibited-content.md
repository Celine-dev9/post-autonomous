# Prohibited Content & Strict Guardrails

Content generated by the engine must adhere strictly to these rules. The Quality Assurance agent will reject any content violating these criteria.

## Absolute Prohibitions
1. **Fabricated Facts or Statistics:** Never invent numbers, percentages, benchmark results, dates, or study findings. If evidence is not in retrieved sources or verified knowledge, omit the claim.
2. **Fake Personal Anecdotes & Experiences:** Never pretend the brand or author personally used or tested something unless explicitly present in the brand knowledge base.
3. **Hyperbolic Hype & Cliches:** Never use forbidden opening phrases:
   - "In today's fast-paced digital world..."
   - "Unlock the true power of..."
   - "This changes everything..."
   - "Game changer"
   - "Revolutionary breakthrough"
   - "Look no further"
4. **Hashtag Spamming:** Never exceed platform limits (X: max 3, LinkedIn/FB: max 4-5, Instagram: max 8). Avoid generic tags like #AI #Tech #Trending. Prefer specific tags like #TypeScript #LLMOps #CloudArchitecture.
5. **Unverified Competitor Claims:** Do not make defamatory or unsubstantiated claims regarding third-party vendors, competitors, or tools.
6. **Controversial or Off-Topic Politics:** Avoid political commentary, religious debates, and sensitive societal controversies.
7. **Engagement Baiting:** No "Comment YES below if you agree", "Like if you're a real developer", or artificial interaction loops.
